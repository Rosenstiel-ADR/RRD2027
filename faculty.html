For Faculty

Meet the Faculty

Meet the Faculty is a standalone 1-minute oral session. No abstract required. Every participating faculty member gives a 1-slide, 1-minute presentation: who you are and a snapshot of your research. Want to also present research? Submit an abstract on the Submit Abstract page.

How this session works

  • One minute, hard stop. A single slide auto-advances after 60 seconds, so the round stays brisk.
  • Self-definition opening. Open with one line on who you are, then a snapshot of your research and what you're looking for (collaborators, students, data).
  • One slide. Send a single slide. A template will be provided after you sign up.
  • Dedicated rounds. Meet the Faculty runs in its own dedicated session during the day, and you'll be placed in one round. Timing will be announced with the program in February.

Other ways faculty take part

  • Present research. Beyond Meet the Faculty, you're welcome to submit an abstract.
  • Mentor & chair. Encourage your team to submit, and help chair a session.
  • Vote on the awards. Vote alongside all attendees in the post-event survey. Winners are announced shortly after the survey closes.
  • Show up for the posters. The poster blocks are where student and postdoc presenters most value faculty attention.

Have students or postdocs presenting?

Students, postdocs, and staff present an oral talk, a lightning talk, or a poster, and they register by submitting an abstract on the Submit Abstract page. Point your group there.
